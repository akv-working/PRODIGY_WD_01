Active Projects

Key field projects currently making an impact.

Coral Nursery Revival

Growing heat-resilient coral micro-fragments to replant degraded reefs in the Indo-Pacific.

River Plastic Barriers

Deploying solar barrier grids in estuaries to intercept plastic before it hits open water.

Seagrass Blue Carbon

Mapping and replanting shallow seagrass meadows that capture carbon and shelter marine life.
